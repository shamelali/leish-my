# Leish — Brand Positioning Brief

## Positioning Statement
For clients in Malaysia who want to hire a makeup artist for an occasion, **Leish** is the client-first beauty booking marketplace that replaces risky, informal Instagram DM hiring with portfolio-verified discovery and escrow-protected booking — unlike StyleSeat, Booksy, and GlossGenius, which are built to manage professionals, not to help clients *find and trust* one.

## Target Customer
Women 18–38 in Klang Valley (expanding across Malaysia) booking makeup for a specific occasion — graduation, birthday glam, content shoot, wedding — who currently rely on screenshots, DMs, and word-of-mouth with no way to verify quality or guarantee payment safety before the appointment.

## Competitive Differentiation
| Competitor | Built For | Leish's Edge |
|---|---|---|
| StyleSeat / Booksy / GlossGenius | The professional (scheduling, CRM, payments *for MUAs*) | Built for the client's discovery and trust journey first |
| Instagram DM booking (status quo) | No structure at all | Portfolio verification, calendar booking, escrow payment |

Leish owns the **client-first, occasion-based discovery** position — no major competitor is fighting for this ground, and none has a TikTok-native discovery strategy.

## Core Customer Emotion to Trigger
**Confidence** — "I know exactly what I'm getting before I book." Trust and ease are the supporting emotions that get the client there; confidence is the payoff.

## Brand Personality (3 traits)
1. **Confident** — decisive, direct, no guesswork language ("Stop scrolling. Start booking.")
2. **Warm** — approachable, never clinical or transactional despite handling payments and trust
3. **Aspirational-but-attainable** — editorial beauty-magazine polish, without gatekeeping or intimidation

## Brand Voice in One Line
*Your stylish best friend who always knows the best MUA in town.*
