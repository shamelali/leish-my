# Leish Pitch Deck
**The Premier Beauty Booking Marketplace in Malaysia**

## Slide 1: Title
- **Headline:** Leish.my
- **Sub-headline:** The Premier Beauty Booking Marketplace in Malaysia.
- **Note:** Seed Funding Presentation

## Slide 2: The Problem
- **Fragmented Market:** Artists rely on Instagram DMs and manual WhatsApp calendars.
- **Lost Revenue:** No-shows and double-bookings destroy margins.
- **Client Friction:** Customers lack a single place to compare prices, read reviews, and book instantly.

## Slide 3: The Leish Solution
- **For Clients:** A fast, secure marketplace to discover and book artists.
- **For Artists:** "Leish Hub" - an all-in-one business dashboard for scheduling and payments.
- **Tech Advantage:** Next.js and Supabase ensure instant load times and extreme security.

## Slide 4: The Market & Economics
- **Premium Bookings:** Average Booking Value (ABV) starts at RM 380.
- **Unmatched Economics:** Customer Acquisition Cost is RM 140. Lifetime Value is RM 2,918.
- **LTV:CAC Ratio:** 20.8x (Massive ROI on marketing spend).

## Slide 5: Business Model
- **Commission Model:** We take a 16% transaction fee in Year 1.
- **Scaling Growth:** Take rate scales to 20% by Year 5 with premium features.
- **Alignment:** Zero upfront risk for artists. We win when they win.
