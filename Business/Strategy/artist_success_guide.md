# Leish! Artist Success Guide: How to Get More Bookings

Welcome to the Leish! elite network. Our mission is to connect Malaysia’s most talented artists with clients who value luxury and quality. Use this guide to optimize your profile and maximize your earnings.

---

## 1. Perfect Your Portfolio (The First Impression)
Clients book with their eyes. Your portfolio is your storefront.
- **Hero Image:** Choose your most "iconic" look as your profile cover. It should be clean, well-lit, and represent your signature style.
- **Diverse Styles:** Show variety. If you do Bridal, include a "Natural Radiance" look and a "Full Glam" look.
- **Lighting is Everything:** High-resolution photos taken in natural light or with a ring light perform 3x better than blurry or dark photos.

## 2. Master Your Availability
Leish! is built on real-time booking. Nothing frustrates a luxury client more than a "Rejected" booking.
- **Keep it Fresh:** Update your calendar weekly. 
- **Block Off Personal Time:** Use the dashboard to mark your off-days or busy hours immediately so you don't get double-booked.
- **Response Time:** Even though it's instant booking, if a client chats with you, aim to reply within 1 hour. Pro-responsiveness builds trust.

## 3. Detail Your Services
Be specific about what the client is paying for.
- **Service Breakdown:** Instead of just "Makeup," list "Bridal Reception (Inc. Hair)" or "Event Glam (Inc. Lashes)."
- **Travel Fees:** If you travel to the client, clearly state your travel radius and any additional transport charges.
- **Duration:** Tell the client how long the session takes (e.g., "90 mins") so they can plan their event day.

## 4. Craft a Compelling Bio
Clients want to know who is behind the brushes.
- **Experience:** "8+ years in bridal and editorial beauty."
- **Philosophy:** "I believe in enhancing natural features rather than masking them."
- **Credentials:** Mention any certifications, famous clients, or major publications you’ve worked with.

## 5. The "Post-Glow" Protocol
Reviews are your currency on Leish!
- **Ask for the Review:** After the session, send a quick message: *"I loved working on your look today! If you enjoyed the experience, I'd love it if you could leave a quick review on my Leish! profile."*
- **Photoshoot Ready:** If the client is comfortable, take a quick "After" photo for your own portfolio (and tag them if you post it!).
