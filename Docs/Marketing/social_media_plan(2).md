# leish.my 7-Day Social Media Launch Plan (Instagram/TikTok)

**Goal:** Build awareness, establish luxury brand positioning, and drive artist sign-ups/customer bookings.
**Handle Suggestion:** @leish.my
**Core Theme:** "Your Beauty, Perfected. Anywhere."

---

## Pre-Launch Countdown (7 Days)

### Day 1: The Teaser (The "Wait, What is This?" Phase)
- **Format:** Reel/Short Video
- **Visual:** High-end, close-up shots of makeup brushes, a model’s eyes, and "The Glow."
- **Audio:** Sophisticated, minimalist lo-fi beat.
- **Hook:** "Something beautiful is coming to Malaysia."
- **Caption:** 
  > A new standard of luxury beauty is arriving. ✨
  >
  > Stay tuned for a seamless way to book the country’s most talented makeup artists.
  >
  > #LeishMY #ComingSoon #LuxuryBeauty #MalaysiaMUA

### Day 2: The Problem/Solution (The "Why Leish?" Phase)
- **Format:** Carousel
- **Slide 1:** "Struggling to find the perfect MUA for your big event?"
- **Slide 2:** "Endless DMs, checking availability, and hunting for portfolios..."
- **Slide 3:** "Meet Leish. Browse, compare, and book in minutes."
- **Slide 4:** Value props: Curated Artists, Real-time Availability, Secure Payments.
- **Caption:** 
  > Booking beauty should be as effortless as the look itself. 💄
  >
  > #BeautySimplified #LeishMY #KualaLumpurMUA #BridalMakeup

### Day 3: Meet the Artists (The Social Proof Phase)
- **Format:** Carousel / Single Post
- **Visual:** Feature 3 artists (Aiko, Mei Lin, Kenji) with their best work.
- **Caption:** 
  > Artistry at its peak. Meet the first wave of curated MUAs joining the Leish! movement. 
  > 
  > From bridal elegance to editorial glam, find your perfect match.
  >
  > #ArtistSpotlight #MUALife #SelangorMUA #LeishArtists

### Day 4: "How It Works" (The Educational Phase)
- **Format:** Reel (Screen recording of the site)
- **Action:** Show a user scrolling through leish.my, clicking an artist, and hitting "Book."
- **Caption:** 
  > 1. Discover. 2. Book. 3. Glow. ✨
  >
  > Luxury beauty at your fingertips. See how easy it is to secure your session.
  >
  > #HowToLeish #BeautyTech #MalaysiaStartup

### Day 5: The "Artist Call" (The Supply Growth Phase)
- **Format:** Graphic / Text-heavy Carousel
- **Message:** "Calling all top-tier MUAs. Grow your business with Leish!"
- **Benefits:** No admin stress, automatic bookings, professional profile.
- **Caption:** 
  > Ready to elevate your career? Join the elite network of Leish! artists. 
  > 
  > Link in bio to apply. 
  >
  > #MUAMalaysia #MakeupArtistKualaLumpur #BeautyEntrepreneur

### Day 6: The Final Countdown (The FOMO Phase)
- **Format:** Reel / Story with Countdown Sticker
- **Visual:** A montage of models and satisfied client testimonials.
- **Hook:** "24 Hours to Go."
- **Caption:** 
  > The wait is almost over. Get ready to transform your beauty routine. ⏳
  >
  > Set your alarms for [Time].
  >
  > #Countdown #LeishLaunch #MalaysiaBeauty

### Day 7: LAUNCH DAY (The Action Phase)
- **Format:** "We Are Live" High-Energy Reel
- **Message:** "LEISH.MY IS LIVE! Book your first session now."
- **Offer:** "Use code **LAUNCH20** for RM20 off your first booking."
- **Caption:** 
  > Your beauty, perfected. Anywhere. 
  >
  > Leish! is officially open for bookings. Visit the link in our bio to find your artist today. 🥂✨
  >
  > #LeishIsLive #GrandOpening #MalaysiaEvents #MakeupBooking

## Ongoing Content Pillars
1. **The Lookbook:** Reposting high-quality work from booked sessions.
2. **Behind the Scenes:** Artists prepping for big weddings/shoots.
3. **Beauty Tips:** Quick 15-second "pro tips" from Leish! MUAs.
